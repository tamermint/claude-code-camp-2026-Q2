## Danger Zones & Monsters
- **Fidos / Scavengers**: Main Street (Low level / Neutral)
- **Newbie Zone (Upper)**: Creepy Crawlers ("perfect match" for L1), Newbie Monster ("needs luck"), Newbie Alchemist ("feel lucky punk")
- **Massive Minotaur Lair**: Newbie Zone -> The Alchemist's Room -> Down (`d`). Sign warns: *"If you are below level 7 and alone, or below level 4 then bugger off! Or else don't blame me if you die..."*
- **Dark Well**: Down from Warrior Practice Yard (Unexplored)
